The story

Some doors should stay closed.
Some secrets won't.

Alena Emerald has made it to Blackwood University with her closest friends by her side and a whole new world waiting beyond its gates. Between classes, basketball, and the bright promise of campus life, everything feels like a beginning.

Then an investigation draws Alena and her friends into the orbit of the Ashford family, where old loyalties, powerful ambitions, and unsettling questions are impossible to ignore. The deeper they look, the more they risk—and the harder it becomes to know whom to trust.

As friendship is tested and an unexpected connection takes root, Alena must decide how far she is willing to go for the truth. Emerald World brings campus life, close-knit friendships, family intrigue, and romantic tension together in one story of secrets and discovery.
